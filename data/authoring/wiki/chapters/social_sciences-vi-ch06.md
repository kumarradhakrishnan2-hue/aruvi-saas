# The Beginnings of Indian Civilisation

**Social Sciences · Class 6 · Chapter 6**

*Defines what makes a civilisation and traces the Sindhu-Sarasvatī (Harappan) civilisation from river-valley villages to planned cities with drainage, wells and reservoirs, through its crafts, food and trade, to its decline as rainfall fell and the Sarasvatī dried.*

> This section introduces the concept of civilisation as an advanced stage of human societies. The chapter defines a civilisation as requiring at least seven key characteristics: some form of government and administration to manage complex society; urbanism including town-planning, city growth, and water and drainage management; a variety of crafts involving raw material management and production of finished goods; both internal and external trade to exchange goods; some form of writing for record-keeping and …
