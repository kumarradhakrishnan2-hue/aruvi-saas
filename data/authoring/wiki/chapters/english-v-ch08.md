# The Decision of the Panchayat

**English · Class 5 · Chapter 8**

*A cunning neighbour sells a farmer a well but refuses him its water, and the village Panchayat outwits him with a judgment that the water in another's well belongs to the well's owner.*

> A hardworking farmer in a small village prospers and buys a third field along with the well next to it from his cunning neighbour. The next day the neighbour refuses to let the farmer draw water from the well, arguing that he sold only the well structure and not the water inside it. The confused farmer approaches the village chief, who convenes a Panchayat meeting under the banyan tree in the village centre. Both parties are summoned and heard.
