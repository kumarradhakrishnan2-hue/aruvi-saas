# Shaping of the Earth's Surface

**Social Sciences · Class 9 · Chapter 2**

*Explains how internal forces (plate tectonics) build the Earth's surface and how external agents — running water, glaciers, waves, wind and groundwater — weather, erode and deposit to level it, ending with landform-linked disasters.*

> The chapter opens by establishing that the Earth's surface is not fixed but is constantly transformed by powerful forces acting both from within the planet and on its surface, with the theory of plate tectonics offered as the central idea that explains these changes. It defines a landform as a natural feature on the Earth's surface produced by processes such as weathering, erosion, deposition, and the movement of the crust, giving mountains, valleys, plateaus, plains, deserts, and coastal features as examples. …
