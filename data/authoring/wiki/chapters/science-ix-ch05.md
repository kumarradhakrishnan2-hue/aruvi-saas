# Exploring Mixtures and their Separation

**Science · Class 9 · Chapter 5**

*Mixtures are classified as homogeneous or heterogeneous and separated by crystallization, distillation, chromatography, separating funnel, sublimation, centrifugation and coagulation, with solutions, suspensions and colloids told apart by the Tyndall effect.*

> Mixtures are classified on the basis of their uniformity into two broad categories: homogeneous and heterogeneous. A homogeneous mixture, also called a solution, has a uniform composition throughout — a well-stirred sugar–water mixture tastes equally sweet from first sip to last. Examples include vinegar (acetic acid in water) and aerated drinks (carbon dioxide in water).
