**Social Sciences · Class 8 · Chapter 10**

*A guided journey through representative Indian structures — Harappan cities, stupas, rock-cut caves, water structures, temples, mosques, forts and palaces, tombs, traditional houses, churches and colonial buildings — and the thought behind them.*

> This chapter takes students on a guided journey through Indian architecture, defined as the art and techniques of design and construction combining functional and aesthetic aspects — a field that is both artistic and highly technical, and in which India has excelled for several millennia.
