# Earth as a System: Energy, Matter, and Life

**Science · Class 9 · Chapter 13**

*Earth is one system of five spheres — geosphere, hydrosphere, cryosphere, atmosphere, biosphere — linked by uneven solar heating, winds and ocean currents, biogeochemical cycles and human impact.*

> This chapter integrates ideas about energy flow, atmospheric processes, biogeochemical cycles, and human impact that students explored separately in earlier grades, presenting them as parts of one interconnected Earth system made up of five interacting spheres: the geosphere (solid rocks, soil, and landforms), hydrosphere (surface water and groundwater), cryosphere (ice and snow), atmosphere (the surrounding air), and biosphere (all living organisms and their habitats).
