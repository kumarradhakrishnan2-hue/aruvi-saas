# Our Home: Earth, a Unique Life Sustaining Planet

**Science · Class 8 · Chapter 13**

*Earth's position, size, atmosphere and magnetic field keep water liquid and life possible, sustained by the interplay of hydrosphere, geosphere, biosphere and atmosphere and now threatened by the triple planetary crisis.*

> This section introduces Earth as the only known planet that supports life in all its forms, situating it as the focus of the concluding chapter of the middle-stage science journey. It establishes that all life exists within a remarkably thin layer on Earth's surface—the crust—which, if Earth were the size of an apple, would be no thicker than the apple's skin.
