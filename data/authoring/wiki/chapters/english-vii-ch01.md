# The Day the River Spoke (Learning Together)

**English · Class 7 · Chapter 1**

*In Kamala Nair's story a personified River counsels Jahnavi, a coastal-village girl kept home from school, to slip into class and claim her right to learn.*

> Jahnavi is a bright girl of nearly ten living in a coastal village in India. Her parents work on the farm, and she has been kept home to mind her younger siblings — first Ramu, then little Appu — while her brother Gopi (whom she calls Ettan, meaning 'Elder brother') and neighbour Meena attend school. Sitting by the river one day, weeping silently as a kingfisher swoops and a lizard basks, Jahnavi hears a sleepy, murmuring voice that turns out to be the River itself.
