# Filling and Lifting

**Mathematics · Class 3 · Chapter 11**

*Introduces capacity by comparing and pouring between vessels, names the litre (with half and quarter litre) as its standard unit, then introduces weight comparison, the kilogram and pan-balance reasoning.*

> This section introduces capacity comparison by asking children to observe that different-shaped vessels hold different amounts of liquid. Through the story of Ritu, Monu, and Nita with glasses of different sizes, children learn that 'how much a container holds' (capacity) cannot be judged by appearance alone. The concrete method — pouring contents into identical same-sized glasses — establishes the idea of a fair comparison.
