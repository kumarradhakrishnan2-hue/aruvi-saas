# Landforms and Life

**Social Sciences · Class 6 · Chapter 3**

*Classifies the Earth's surface into mountains, plateaus and plains (plus deserts), describing each landform's environment, the life and livelihoods it supports, and the challenges and cultural meanings people attach to it.*

> The chapter opens by establishing that land takes many forms across the Earth's surface. A journey from Chhota Nagpur in Jharkhand to Prayagraj in Uttar Pradesh and on to Almora in Uttarakhand, for instance, passes through three distinctly different landscapes. A landform is defined as a physical feature on the Earth's surface, shaped over millions of years and deeply connected to the environment and life.
