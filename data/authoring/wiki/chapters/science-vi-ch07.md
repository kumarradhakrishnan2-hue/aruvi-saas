# Temperature and its Measurement

**Science · Class 6 · Chapter 7**

*Touch is an unreliable judge of hot and cold, so temperature is measured with clinical, laboratory and room thermometers on the Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin scales.*

> This section establishes that our sense of touch is unreliable as a measure of hotness or coldness. Using a narrative about Lambok and his sister Phiban, the chapter opens with the question of whether fever can always be detected by touch alone, prompting the need for a more reliable method.
