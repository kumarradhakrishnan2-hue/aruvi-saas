# Weight and Capacity

**Mathematics · Class 5 · Chapter 8**

*Formalises weight units from milligram to tonne and capacity in litres and millilitres, with double-number-line conversion, comparison and all four operations on compound quantities.*

> The chapter opens with a real-life context: Anu has recorded weights of household items (iron almirah, bed, sofa, refrigerator, etc.) and students must tick those that are plausible. This section activates prior sense of the gram–kilogram distinction by asking learners to evaluate whether recorded values (e.g. 40 g for an iron almirah) are reasonable. The task is purely perceptual-reasoning: no calculation is required, only familiarity with everyday objects and appropriate units.
