# Weigh It, Pour It

**Mathematics · Class 4 · Chapter 8**

*Revisits weight and capacity from Grade 3, introduces grams and millilitres as sub-units with their fraction relationships to the kilogram and litre, and closes with measuring everyday water use and waste.*

> The chapter opens by revisiting weight and capacity from Grade 3, then situates learning in a vegetable market scene where a seller weighs vegetables. Students compare animals by heaviness, reflect on objects they interact with daily (school bag, heaviest book), and predict the weight of a half pumpkin using a scale image. The section moves along the concrete path — students draw on lived experience and visual estimation before any formal measurement.
