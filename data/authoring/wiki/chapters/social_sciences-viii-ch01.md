# Natural Resources and Their Use

**Social Sciences · Class 8 · Chapter 1**

*Explains when Nature becomes a resource, categorises resources by use and renewability, shows how uneven distribution shapes settlement and conflict, and argues for stewardship through cases of groundwater over-extraction, cement and organic farming.*

> The chapter distinguishes between Nature and natural resources. Nature comprises life and non-life forms in our environment not created by humans; elements of Nature become 'resources' when humans use them for sustenance or to create goods — a tree becomes a resource when its wood is made into furniture. For any entity to qualify as a resource, three conditions must be met: it must be technologically accessible, economically feasible to exploit, and culturally acceptable.
