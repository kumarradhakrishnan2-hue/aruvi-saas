# Nature's Treasures

**Science · Class 6 · Chapter 11**

*Air, water, sunlight, forests, soil, rocks, minerals and fossil fuels are natural resources, classified as renewable or non-renewable and to be used responsibly.*

> Section 11.1 introduces air as a vital natural resource essential for the survival of all living beings. Students perform a breathing exercise (Activity 11.1) to experience first-hand how quickly the body requires oxygen—holding the breath causes discomfort because the body cannot function without it. Air is described as a mixture of gases that surrounds the Earth: nitrogen makes up 78%, oxygen 21%, and argon, carbon dioxide, and other gases together account for the remaining 1%.
