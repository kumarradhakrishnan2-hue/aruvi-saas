# Atmosphere and Climate

**Social Sciences · Class 9 · Chapter 3**

*Describes the atmosphere's composition and layers, separates weather from climate and sets out their elements, explains India's seasons and the monsoon's reversal, and closes with climate change and a case study of the 2025 Punjab floods.*

> The chapter opens by introducing the atmosphere as the blanket of air held around the Earth by gravity, a mixture of gases in varying proportions that is vital for the survival of all living beings. It explains that the atmosphere shields the planet from harmful solar radiation, including ultraviolet radiation, regulates temperature by trapping some of the Sun's energy and preventing it from escaping to space, and is a key component of the Earth's weather and climate systems, influencing temperature, humidity, and …
