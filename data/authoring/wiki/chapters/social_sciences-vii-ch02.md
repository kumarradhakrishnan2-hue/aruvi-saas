# Understanding the Weather

**Social Sciences · Class 7 · Chapter 2**

*Defines weather and its five elements — temperature, precipitation, pressure, wind and humidity — explains how each is measured, and shows how weather stations and the IMD turn those readings into forecasts and warnings.*

> Weather is defined as the state of the Earth's atmosphere at a particular time and place. The atmosphere is the layer of gases surrounding the Earth, commonly referred to as air, and it is structured in multiple layers like a cake. The layer closest to the Earth's surface is called the troposphere, which extends from 6 to 18 kilometres above the ground — thinner at the poles where cold air contracts, and thicker in the tropical zone where warmer air expands.
