# Madhu's Wish

**English · Class 3 · Chapter 13**

*A boy who shares his lunch is granted a wish by a magical banyan tree, asks for endless food and no water, and learns through unstoppable hiccups that water matters more than any feast.*

> Madhu, a boy who loves eating all kinds of food, sits under a banyan tree every day at noon for lunch. One day an old man arrives; Madhu shares his food. The grateful old man grants Madhu a wish from the magical tree. Madhu wishes for different types of food forever, ignoring the tree's gentle warning to think carefully.
