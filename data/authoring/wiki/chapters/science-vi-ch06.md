# Materials Around Us

**Science · Class 6 · Chapter 6**

*Objects are made of materials that can be grouped by observable properties — lustre, hardness, transparency, solubility in water, mass and volume — leading to the definition of matter as anything that occupies space and has mass.*

> This section introduces students to the idea that all objects around us are made from materials such as paper, wood, cloth, glass, metal, plastic, and clay. Through a classroom conversation between characters Ghulan, Sheeta, and their teacher Madam Vidya, students are prompted to notice and compare the objects they bring to school every day. The key term introduced is material, defined as any substance used to create an object. The section includes Activity 6.1, a listing activity where students identify objects …
