# Pressure, Winds, Storms, and Cyclones

**Science · Class 8 · Chapter 6**

*Pressure — in solids, liquids and the atmosphere — explains winds, sea and land breezes, storms, lightning and cyclones.*

> This section introduces the concept of pressure as force per unit area, motivated by a relatable scenario of two children carrying bags with narrow and broad straps. The key term introduced is pressure, defined as Pressure = Force / Area. The section establishes that when the same force is distributed over a smaller area, the resulting pressure is higher — which is why narrow-strap bags feel more uncomfortable than broad-strap bags. The SI unit of pressure is newton per metre squared (N/m²), also called pascal …
