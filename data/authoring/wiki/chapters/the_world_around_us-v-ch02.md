# Journey of a River

**The World Around Us · Class 5 · Chapter 2**

*The Godavari narrates its own journey from source to delta, showing how a river supports life, is dammed, polluted, floods and runs dry, and what people owe it in return.*

## Water ideas in this chapter

| Idea | Role | What this chapter says | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[water-as-solvent|Water as a solvent]] | ● introduces | Some pollutants dissolve invisibly in water while others such as oil do not (a spoon of sugar versus a spoon of cooking oil stirred into two glasses), both harming the river, and students design a simple water filter from everyday materials. | Section: What Pollutes Me and Why it Matters / T-13; Let us reflect / T-26 |
| [[rivers-and-landforms|Rivers and landforms]] | ◐ extends | One river is followed source to sea: the Godavari rises as a stream at Trimbakeshwar in the Western Ghats, gathers tributaries over 1,465 km, flows year-round (perennial, unlike seasonal rivers), spreads into a delta where freshwater meets the sea at the Coringa mangroves, and overflows its banks in floods when rain is excessive — worse where forests have been cut. | Sections: Where I Begin, Where I Flow / The Life I Hold / When a River Floods (T-1, T-16) |
| [[water-as-resource|Water as a resource]] | ◐ extends | A river supplies water for drinking, cooking, washing, irrigation and industry through pipes and canals; India's 900-plus dams store river water for dry seasons, irrigation and electricity at the cost of flooding land and displacing people; and drought (Chennai 2019) forces migration and calls for conservation habits, the National Water Mission and water ATMs. | Sections: The Many Ways I Support Lives / Dams—Storing My Water, Changing My Flow / When a River Runs Dry! (T-3, T-6, T-8, T-18, T-19) |
| [[water-and-life|Water and life]] | ◐ extends | Waste, plastic, untreated sewage and fertiliser runoff make river water unsafe for people and wildlife — algal 'green blankets' choke aquatic life and make water undrinkable — and floods spread disease and cut off safe drinking water. | Section: What Pollutes Me and Why it Matters / T-12, T-14, T-15; When a River Floods / T-16 |
| [[water-in-society|Water in society]] | ◐ extends | Since ancient times settlements have grown beside rivers for reliable water; the Godavari is sacred (Dakshina Ganga, the Pushkaram festival at Nashik), families carry river stories and festivals, and students role-play as Sarpanch or plan a riverside city that stays clean, flood-safe and supplied through summer. | Sections: Where I Begin, Where I Flow / The Many Ways I Support Lives / Let us reflect (T-2, T-17, T-21, T-23, T-27) |
| [[rain-and-monsoon|Rain and the monsoon]] | ○ uses | Rainfall governs a river's year: seasonal rivers flow only in the rainy season, excessive rain makes rivers overflow their banks, and dams hold water back for the dry season. | Sections: The Life I Hold / Dams—Storing My Water, Changing My Flow / When a River Floods |
