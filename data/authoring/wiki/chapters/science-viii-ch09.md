# The Amazing World of Solutes, Solvents, and Solutions

**Science · Class 8 · Chapter 9**

*Solutions are defined through solute, solvent, saturation, concentration and solubility (of solids and gases with temperature); then density and its measurement explain floating and sinking.*

> This section introduces the distinction between uniform and non-uniform mixtures, using everyday examples such as salt-in-water versus sand-in-water. A uniform mixture of two or more substances is defined as a solution. When a solid dissolves in a liquid to form a solution, the solid component is called the solute and the liquid is called the solvent.
