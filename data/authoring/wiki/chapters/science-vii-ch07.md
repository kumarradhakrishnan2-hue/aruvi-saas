# Heat Transfer in Nature

**Science · Class 7 · Chapter 7**

*Heat moves by conduction, convection and radiation, and these modes explain land and sea breezes, the water cycle and the seepage of rainwater into the ground.*

> 7.1 Conduction of Heat establishes through an experimental investigation that heat travels along a metal strip from its hotter end towards its cooler end. The process of heat transfer from the hotter part of an object to the cooler part, where the particles themselves do not move from their positions but pass heat on to neighbouring particles, is defined as conduction.
