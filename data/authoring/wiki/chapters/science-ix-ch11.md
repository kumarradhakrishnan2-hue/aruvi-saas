# Reproduction: How Life Continues

**Science · Class 9 · Chapter 11**

*Asexual and sexual reproduction in plants, animals and humans, from vegetative propagation and meiosis to pollination, fertilisation, pregnancy and reproductive health.*

> 11.1 Asexual Reproduction Asexual reproduction is found in many unicellular organisms such as bacteria, amoeba, and yeast, as well as in simple multicellular organisms like hydra and sponge, and in many plants. In plants, new individuals arise from vegetative parts without seeds — for example, potato and ginger sprout from fleshy underground stems, money plant and sugarcane grow from stem cuttings, and Bryophyllum leaves produce tiny plantlets.
