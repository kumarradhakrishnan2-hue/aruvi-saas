# Particulate Nature of Matter

**Science · Class 8 · Chapter 7**

*All matter is made of tiny constituent particles whose spacing, attraction and motion explain the solid, liquid and gaseous states and the changes between them.*

> Section 7.1 introduces the central question of what matter is made up of. Through Activity 7.1 (breaking and grinding chalk), students explore whether matter can be subdivided indefinitely. The activity establishes that even after grinding chalk to fine powder, each speck remains chalk — a physical change only. The key term introduced is constituent particle: the basic indivisible unit that makes up a larger piece of a substance. Activity 7.2 (dissolving sugar in water) reinforces this idea.
