# A Journey through States of Water

**Science · Class 6 · Chapter 8**

*Water exists as ice, liquid water and invisible water vapour; heating and cooling move it between these states, and evaporation and condensation at planetary scale give clouds and rain.*

> This section opens with children noticing that water from playground puddles disappears after rain. It introduces the concept that water on surfaces—utensils, mopped floors, wet clothes—dries up over time, prompting students to investigate whether water seeps through surfaces or disappears in another way. Through an activity using a steel plate, students establish that water does not seep through a solid surface. The section introduces the key term evaporation: the process of conversion of water into its vapour …
