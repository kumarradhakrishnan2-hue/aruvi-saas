# World Geography: Some Glimpses

**Social Sciences · Class 8 · Chapter 8**

*A guided tour of the Earth's physical features — from map projections to the oceans, currents, trenches and reefs, then continent by continent through plateaus, deserts, steppes, mountains, plains and rivers — and the human activities and environmental pressures tied to each.*

> This chapter takes students on a guided tour of the Earth's major physical features, moving from the challenge of representing the planet on paper, to the oceans, and then continent by continent across the world's landforms.
