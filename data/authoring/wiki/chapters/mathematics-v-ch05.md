# Far and Near

**Mathematics · Class 5 · Chapter 5**

*Builds the length unit hierarchy from millimetre to kilometre and performs conversions and the four operations on compound lengths.*

> The chapter opens by activating prior knowledge of length units — metre and centimetre — through a matching activity. Students identify which unit is appropriate for everyday quantities such as the height of India Gate, the length of a handkerchief, or the depth of a well. This is a concrete-to-symbolic warm-up that anchors unit sense before new units are introduced.

## Water ideas in this chapter

*None recorded.* Length and distance only; water stations in a race, a well's depth and a waterfall's distance appear as incidental context, never as measured or discussed water.
