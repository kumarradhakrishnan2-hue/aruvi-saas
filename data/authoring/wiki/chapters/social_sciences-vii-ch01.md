# Geographical Diversity of India

**Social Sciences · Class 7 · Chapter 1**

*Tours India's physical regions — Himalayas, Ladakh, Gangetic plains, Thar, Aravallis, peninsular plateau, coasts, islands, the Sundarbans and the Northeast hills — and the life, resources and culture each supports.*

> India is the seventh-largest country in the world and forms part of Asia. Along with its neighbours — Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Bhutan, Sri Lanka, and Myanmar — it constitutes the Indian Subcontinent. The Himalayan Mountain range stands as a natural barrier in the north, the Thar Desert and Arabian Sea mark its western limits, the Indian Ocean lies to the south, and the Bay of Bengal to the east.
