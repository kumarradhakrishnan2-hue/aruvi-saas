**Science · Class 9 · Chapter 2**

*Cells are studied through the microscope, their membrane and wall, their organelles, cell division by mitosis and meiosis, and the Cell Theory.*

> Section 2.1 introduces the challenge of studying cells given their sub-millimetre size. The human eye can resolve two points only if they are at least 0.1 mm apart — the limit of resolution — which means most plant and animal cells, lying in the range of 10–100 µm, require optical assistance to study. Robert Hooke first observed cells in 1665 using a self-designed microscope with 200–300× magnification, identifying box-like compartments in cork.
