# Water — a concept thread through the curriculum

*Pilot wiki built from Aruvi's chapter summaries. One theme, eleven ideas, 48 chapters across five subjects and Classes 3–9.*

## How to read this

Every **idea** has a page listing each chapter that touches it, in class order, with one sentence saying what that chapter adds. Every **chapter** has a page listing the ideas it touches. Three views read the same links three ways: [[spiral-water|the spiral]] (one idea, class by class), [[joint-classes|joint classes]] (same class, two subjects, one idea) and [[cross-subject|cross-subject connections]] (which subjects lean on which).

Role marks: ● introduces · ◐ extends · ○ uses.

## Looked at, left out

The keyword scan flagged these, the reader found only incidental water content:

- Mathematics Class 5 ch 5 *Far and Near* — Length and distance only; water stations in a race, a well's depth and a waterfall's distance appear as incidental context, never as measured or discussed water.
- English Class 6 ch 11 *The Winner (Sports and Wellness)* — Water is only the setting ('next to the creek'); the sole water task is a vocabulary word web naming other water bodies (Let us learn), plus a swimming riddle in Let us listen — incidental, neither taught nor substantively used.
- Science Class 8 ch 2 *The Invisible Living World: Beyond Our Naked Eye* — Water appears only as a sampling medium (pond or stagnant water observed under the microscope, a water-filled flask used as a lens) and as the habitat of microalgae; the chapter teaches cells and microorganisms, not any water concept.
- Science Class 9 ch 11 *Reproduction: How Life Continues* — Water appears only incidentally — as the medium of external fertilisation in fish and frogs, one pollination agent among four, and a seed-dispersal and germination condition; the chapter teaches reproduction, not a water concept.
