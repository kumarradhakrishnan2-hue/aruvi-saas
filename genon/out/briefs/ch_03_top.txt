- This is the chapter's fullest plan: 12 units x 50 minutes (period_schedule: exactly one row {50, 12}).
- IT IS SERVED WHOLE AND IN PARTS. A teacher whose budget is smaller receives its first X-1 units followed by a closing unit borrowed from a companion plan of this same chapter. So ANY unit may be the last one she teaches, and any unit may sit beside a unit she has never seen.
- Therefore every unit CLOSES ON ITS OWN GROUND: it names no other unit, promises nothing that follows it, and never claims the chapter has been covered. Where continuity helps, name the CONTENT already taught ("Having traced the Vedic political vocabulary, ...") — never a unit's position or existence.
- MATERIALS, OPENING MOVES AND HOMEWORK ARE PER-UNIT. No unit may require that another unit was taught, or that its homework was set, in order to run: a unit whose first activity collects an earlier unit's homework dead-ends whenever the teacher's budget did not include that unit.
- The final unit closes the chapter as a unit-arc of its own, without announcing that it is doing so.
