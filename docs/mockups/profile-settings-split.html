Profile ⁄ Settings split — proposal

The top bar gains a person icon (👤 = what I teach) beside the gear (⚙ = my account & app). The profile becomes direct manipulation — one card per subject, classes as rows, sections as chips with an ✕, dashed chips to add — no Edit-mode toggle, no dustbin reveal. Settings becomes a grouped list where the subscription, data download and account deletion live. Same warm-paper system throughout.
