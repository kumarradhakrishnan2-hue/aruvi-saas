# Does the unit-level learning arc exist? — a corpus study across five subjects

Run 2026-07-30 for ARV-D-006 (roles) · method: positional marker analysis over every saved plan
in `backup/saved_plans/` plus the two live canonicals · 367 units with ≥3 bands · no API, no tokens.

**Why this corpus is good evidence.** 40 of the 43 backup plans were authored *before*
`role_handoff` existed — under constitutions that never mentioned hook / development /
consolidation. Whatever pattern they show is therefore natural authoring, not a taxonomy the
prompt induced. This is as close to an unprimed sample as the project will ever have.

**Method.** For every unit, each band was tagged by position (first / middle / last) and its
activity text scanned for three marker families: HOOK (interrogatives, ask, recall, predict,
elicit, brainstorm, begin/start by, prior knowledge, introduce), CONSOLIDATION (summarise,
reflect, recap, wrap up, conclude, review, key points, share out, note down), DEVELOPMENT
(read, work through, solve, practise, demonstrate, observe, investigate, analyse, construct,
examine, source, experiment). Band minutes were recorded alongside.

## Results

Share of bands at each position containing a marker of each family:

| Subject | units | HOOK first / mid / last | CONS first / mid / last | DEV first / mid / last |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| english | 74 | **55%** / 26% / 28% | 14% / 14% / 16% | 15% / 18% / 19% |
| mathematics | 87 | **82%** / 49% / 43% | 52% / 13% / 47% | 20% / 35% / 25% |
| science | 60 | **73%** / 30% / 25% | 5% / 13% / **47%** | 20% / 27% / 5% |
| social_sciences | 122 | **89%** / 49% / 60% | 5% / 9% / **35%** | 27% / 57% / 26% |
| the_world_around_us | 24 | **75%** / 37% / 42% | 12% / 4% / 29% | 8% / 19% / 4% |

Mean band minutes by position:

| Subject | first | middle | last |
|---|---|---|---|
| english | 8.6 | 13.5 | 6.0 |
| mathematics | 8.8 | 13.8 | 9.1 |
| science | 7.8 | 13.0 | 6.2 |
| social_sciences | 8.1 | 12.9 | 8.4 |
| the_world_around_us | 6.2 | 10.4 | 5.3 |

## Findings

**1. The opening is real, and it is universal.** Every subject, without exception, opens its
units with hook language, and at 1.7×–2.4× the rate of its own middle bands. This is the one
part of the arc the corpus supports unambiguously — and it is the part the engine actually
depends on, since `CUT_COST[hook] = 6` exists to stop a sitting being cut immediately after a
unit's opening.

**2. The close is weak, and it is NOT universal.** Consolidation language at the last band runs
47% (science), 47% (maths), 35% (SS), 29% (TWAU) — and **16% in english, against 14% at its
first band and 14% at its middles: a completely flat profile.** English units, on this evidence,
do not close; they stop. The founder's hypothesis — that maths or another subject might not
carry the arc — is confirmed, but the subject it fails for is English.

**3. The middle is where time lives, in every subject.** Middle bands run 10.4–13.8 minutes
against 5.3–9.1 at the edges. This is the most consistent regularity in the whole corpus and it
is entirely positional: short opening, long substance, short close.

**4. One confound, stated plainly.** Mathematics shows 52% consolidation markers at the *first*
band — almost certainly "review / recap the previous concept" opening a lesson, which is
functionally a hook. The classifier reads language, not intent, so maths's arc is probably
stronger than the table suggests, not weaker. No other subject shows this inversion.

## What this means for roles

The three-role taxonomy asserts a three-part arc. The corpus supports **one and a half parts of
it**: a genuine opening everywhere, a genuine close in three subjects of five, and a middle that
is defined by being neither. Meanwhile the emitted `role_handoff` in both live canonicals is
100% derivable from band position (33/33 units), and every engine use of roles — `CUT_COST`,
`DEV_PACE_FLOOR`, `DEMOTE_BELOW` — is ordinal in effect.

Under Occam's razor the conclusion is uncomfortable but clean: **position already carries
everything the roles carry.** Where the arc is real (the opening) position marks it; where the
arc is absent (english's close) the role label would be a fiction, and labelling that band
"consolidation" merely tells the engine a unit boundary is a cheap place to cut — which it is,
for structural reasons, regardless.

**Limitation.** This is keyword evidence about language, not adjudicated evidence about
pedagogical function. It can establish that a positional pattern exists and how strong it is per
subject; it cannot settle borderline single-band judgements. A ground-truth check — an LLM
classifying ~60 sampled bands blind to position, then comparing against the positional default —
would cost roughly ₹3 and is the natural follow-up if this study's conclusion is to carry a
constitutional change across eleven files.
